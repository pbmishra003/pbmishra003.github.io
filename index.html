About

The plant was built to run. Nobody built it to be attacked.

Here's the thing about operational technology. Grids, substations and factories were designed for uptime, not for a world where they sit one click away from the internet. Most boards now know that. Very few can tell whether the money they spend on security actually lowers the risk.

That gap is where I work. For more than 16 years I have sat between engineers, buyers and boards, turning standards like IEC 62443 and laws like NIS2 into choices a director can defend in the minutes.

Today I lead global new customer acquisition and commercial strategy at BlackBear TechHive, part of the ATOP group, and I serve on the board of Atech Solutions Pvt Ltd, the group's India company. I am registered on India's Independent Directors Databank.
